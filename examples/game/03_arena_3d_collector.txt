At the start, show "GEMS {score} / 10" at the top left and play the music "E4 G4 B4 E5" at 110 beats per minute as a triangle wave.
Move the player across the ground with the arrow keys at 7 units per second and face where it moves, smoothly.
The player jumps with space at 9 units per second with the jump sound.
Each skeleton moves toward the player across the ground at 2.5 units per second within 10 units.
Each spider wanders across the ground at 3 units per second, turning every 1.2 seconds.
Each ghost shoots at the player at 6 units per second every 2 seconds within 12 units, using "bomb".
When the player touches a gem, destroy it, add 1 to the score, play the pickup sound and play the spin animation on the player.
When a bomb hits the player, destroy the bomb and the player loses.
When the player touches a skeleton, the player loses "CAUGHT".
When no gems are left, the player wins.
When the player falls below -10, the player goes back to the start.
When the player presses enter, restart the game.
