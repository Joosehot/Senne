At the start, the player has 3 lives, show "TIME {countdown 60}" at the top and show "LIVES {lives}  SCORE {score}" at the top left.
Move the player with the arrow keys at 7 units per second and face where it moves.
The player shoots forward with space at 16 units per second every 0.25 seconds, using "shot", with the shoot sound.
Each zombie moves toward the player at 2.3 units per second, speeding up by 1.2 percent per second.
Each ghost moves toward the player at 1.8 units per second within 9 units.
Each blob wanders at 2 units per second, turning every 1.5 seconds.
When a shot hits a zombie, destroy both, add 1 to the score and play the explosion sound.
When a shot hits a blob, destroy both and add 2 points.
When the player touches an enemy, the player loses a life, safe for 1.5 seconds.
When the time reaches 60 seconds, the player wins "YOU SURVIVED".
When the player presses r, restart the game.
