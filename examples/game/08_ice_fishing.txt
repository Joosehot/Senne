At the start, show "SCORE {score} / 50" at the top left in "#16324f", show "TIME {countdown 90}" at the top right in "#16324f" and show the shout meter at the bottom.
The hook hangs on a line from the hole, sinking at 1.5 units per second down to -9.
The hook rises while the player shouts or holds space, at up to 8 units per second as loud as the shout.
Each perch swims within 16 units at 2 units per second.
Each roach swims within 16 units at 3 units per second.
Each pike swims within 16 units at 1.5 units per second.
Each hooked perch follows its hook, fighting and pulling it down at 2 units per second in bursts.
Each hooked roach follows its hook, fighting and pulling it down at 1.5 units per second in bursts.
Each hooked pike follows its hook, fighting and pulling it down at 5 units per second in bursts.
When a hook touches a fish and no fish is hooked, the fish bites, squash it, play the hit sound and show "FISH ON! SHOUT!" in the middle for 1.5 seconds.
When a hooked perch reaches the hole, add 10 points, destroy it, play the splash sound, show "PERCH +10" in the middle for 1.5 seconds and a new perch comes in from the side after 3 seconds.
When a hooked roach reaches the hole, add 5 points, destroy it, play the splash sound, show "ROACH +5" in the middle for 1.5 seconds and a new roach comes in from the side after 2 seconds.
When a hooked pike reaches the hole, add 30 points, destroy it, play the splash sound, show "PIKE +30" in the middle for 1.5 seconds and a new pike comes in from the side after 6 seconds.
When a hooked fish stays at the bottom for 3 seconds, it gets away, play the splash sound and show "IT GOT AWAY!" in the middle for 1.5 seconds.
When the score reaches 50, the player wins "GREAT CATCH".
When the time reaches 90 seconds, the player loses "TIME UP".
When the player presses r, restart the game.
