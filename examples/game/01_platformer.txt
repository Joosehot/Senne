At the start, the player has 3 lives, play the music "C4 E4 G4 C5 G4 E4 C4 -" at 132 beats per minute as a square wave, show "COINS {score} / 14" at the top left and show "LIVES {lives}" at the top right.
Move the player sideways with the arrow keys at 7.5 units per second and face where it moves.
The player jumps with space at 14.5 units per second, twice at 12 units per second, higher while held, with the jump sound.
Animate the player with "hero_1" and "hero_2" at 8 frames per second while it walks.
Each slime patrols 3 units to each side at 2.2 units per second.
Animate each slime with "slime_1" and "slime_2" at 4 frames per second.
Each bat flies in a wave 1.2 units high at 2 cycles per second.
When the player touches a coin, destroy it, add 1 to the score and play the coin sound.
When the player lands on a slime, destroy it, bounce the player up at 11 units per second, squash the player and play the bounce sound.
When the player touches a slime, the player loses a life, safe for 1.5 seconds.
When the player touches a bat, the player loses a life, safe for 1.5 seconds.
When the player falls below -12, the player loses a life and goes back to the start.
When the player touches the flag and no coins are left, the player wins "LEVEL CLEAR".
When the player presses r, restart the game.
